Nutri Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Nutri is designed to keep meal planning private. Dish search, meal-plan generation, and grocery-list generation run on your device. Meal suggestions use a local algorithm; your recipes and planning preferences are not sent to an AI service.

Information Nutri handles

Nutri stores the dishes and ingredients you add, meal plans, preferences, grocery lists, assistant choice, and app settings. This content is stored locally. iCloud synchronization and collaborative sharing are disabled in the current release. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings. Nutri does not operate a separate meal-data server.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives purchase history and a randomly generated anonymous app user identifier, along with product and entitlement status, storefront, and app version. This information is used to verify and restore access, prevent fraud, and understand subscription performance. Nutri does not link it to a personal account, name, or email address. Nutri does not receive your full payment-card details.

Diagnostics and third parties

Apple may process device, backup, StoreKit, and crash information under your Apple settings and Apple’s policies. RevenueCat processes purchase information as described above. Nutri does not sell personal information and does not use third-party advertising trackers.

Local storage and deletion

The current app does not share recipes, plans, or shopping lists with other people. You can edit or delete dishes, ingredients, plans, lists, and list items in the app. Deleting a plan or list keeps your recipes. Removing the app deletes its local data. Backups and any iCloud records from earlier builds can be managed through your Apple settings. Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction records as needed to provide purchases and meet their legal obligations.

Children, security, and changes

Nutri is not directed to children under 13. We use platform security controls and minimize transmitted data, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Material policy changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
